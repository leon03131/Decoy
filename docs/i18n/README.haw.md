# Decoy

**Nā polokalamu pani liʻiliʻi e hoʻolilo iā Discord e manaʻo e holo nei kekahi pāʻani.**

![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Windows%2010%20%7C%2011-7c6cf6)
![Built with](https://img.shields.io/badge/built%20with-Tauri%202%20%2B%20Rust-7c6cf6)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-7c6cf6)

<img src="../screenshots/library.jpg" width="860" alt="Waihona pāʻani Decoy me nā pāʻani hou a me nā kiʻi" />

</div>

Mālama ʻo Decoy i kekahi polokalamu pani pono ʻole me kekahi pukaaniani e holo ana ma lalo o ka inoa hoʻokō o kekahi pāʻani, no laila hōʻike ka ʻōnaehana huli pāʻani a Discord iā ʻoe e pāʻani ana i kēlā pāʻani. ʻAʻole pono ʻoe e hoʻouka a hoʻomaka paha i ka pāʻani maoli, no laila ʻaʻohe mea nui e noho ana ma kāu pāpaho paʻakikī (disk).

Ua ʻike ʻo ia e pili ana i **nā pāʻani a pau hiki iā Discord ke ʻike** (ma kahi o 25,000 mau helu) no ka mea heluhelu ia i kā Discord papa inoa huli lehulehu ponoʻī a hoʻoponopono hou iā ia iho.

> [!WARNING]
> Hiki i ka hoʻopunipuni ʻana i kekahi pāʻani e holo nei ke kūʻē i nā Kūlana Hana o Discord a ke hoʻohana nei ʻoe iā Decoy **ma kou pilikia ponoʻī**.
> ʻAʻole pili kēia papahana me, ʻaʻole i ʻae ʻia e, a ʻaʻole i pili iā Discord Inc. E nānā iā [Hoʻokuʻu kuleana](#hoʻokuʻu-kuleana).

## Nā Hiʻona

- **Nā mea a pau hiki iā Discord ke ʻike.** Hoʻoiho ʻia ka papa inoa pāʻani mai ka wahi huli lehulehu o Discord, mālama ʻia ma kahi kokoke a hoʻoponopono hou ʻia i kēlā me kēia lā. Hōʻike maʻamau ʻia nā pāʻani hou a me nā polokalamu hoʻokō i hoʻohui hou ʻia.
- **Huli wikiwiki.** Huli koke ma ka inoa pāʻani a i ʻole ka inoa exe ma waena o nā tausani pāʻani, ʻike māhele papa a papa inoa paha, nā punahele a me kahi lalani "Nā mea i hala koke iho".
- **Kahi kāomi hoʻokahi.** Koho i kekahi pāʻani, koho i ka polokalamu hoʻokō (inā he nui), koho i ka lōʻihi o ka manawa a kāomi i ka hoʻomaka. E hōʻike ana kekahi apo helu hope a me kekahi hoʻolaha iā ʻoe ke pau ka manawa.
- **ʻAʻole waiho i kekahi mea.** Aia wale nō ka decoy i ka wā e holo ai: haku ʻia ka exe liʻiliʻi i ka wā e hoʻomaka ai a holoi ʻia ke hoʻopau ʻia. Hoʻopau ʻia nā mea a pau ke puka ʻoe, a hoʻomaʻemaʻe ʻia nā koena i waiho ʻia ma hope o kekahi pilikia (crash) i ka hoʻomaka hou ʻana.
- **Mālie.** Noho ma ka pā ʻōnaehana (tray), ʻaʻohe pukaaniani a pihi pahu hana (taskbar) no nā decoy ponoʻī.
- **Nā unuhi hāmama.** Hoʻokele ʻia ka nānaina e nā waihona JSON maʻamau, no laila he hoʻokahi wale nō waihona ka hoʻohui ʻana i kekahi ʻōlelo. Ua hoʻokomo ʻia ka ʻōlelo Pelekānia a me Lūkini.
- **Kūpono pilikino.** ʻAʻohe waihona moʻokāki, ʻaʻohe token Discord, ʻaʻohe telemetry. E nānā iā [Kūpono pilikino](#kūpono-pilikino).

## Pehea e hana ai

1. Manaʻo ʻo Discord e pāʻani ana ʻoe i kekahi pāʻani ma o ka nānā ʻana i nā polokalamu e holo nei ma kāu PC a me ka hoʻohālikelike ʻana i ko lākou mau inoa hoʻokō me kekahi papa inoa lehulehu (`/api/v9/applications/detectable`).
2. Hoʻoiho ʻo Decoy i kēlā papa inoa a, no ka pāʻani āu i koho ai, kope i kekahi polokalamu pani liʻiliʻi (ma kahi o 120 KB, i kākau ʻia ma Rust a i hoʻokomo ʻia i loko o Decoy) i kekahi waihona noho pōkole me ka hoʻohana ʻana i ka inoa hoʻokō mai ka papa inoa, no ka laʻana `.../cod26-cod.exe`.
3. ʻAʻohe hana a ka polokalamu pani koe wale nō ka mālama ʻana i hoʻokahi pukaaniani pae kiʻekiʻe, i hoʻonoho ʻia ma waho loa o ka papakaumaka i ʻike ʻole ai ʻoe. Hoʻomaopopo wale nō ʻo Discord i kekahi polokalamu nona kekahi pukaaniani, ʻo ia ke kumu e nānā ʻole ʻia ai kekahi polokalamu pukaaniani ʻole.
4. I loko o kahi o 15 kekona, hōʻike ʻo Discord i ka pāʻani ma ke ʻano he hana e hana ʻia nei e ʻoe.
5. Ke pau ka manawa a i ʻole ke kāomi ʻoe iā hoʻopau, e hoʻopau ʻo Decoy i ka polokalamu a holoi i ka waihona. Mālama kekahi mea hana Windows job object e make pū nā decoy me Decoy, inā nō he hāʻule (crash) kā Decoy.

## Hoʻouka

Koho i hoʻokahi mai ka ʻaoʻao [Nā Hoʻopuka](../../../../releases):

| Hoʻoiho | No |
|---|---|
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` | **Mea hoʻonoho.** No kēlā me kēia mea hoʻohana, ʻaʻole pono nā kuleana alakaʻi (administrator), hoʻohui i kekahi puka komo i ka papa kuhikuhi Hoʻomaka a me kekahi mea wehe polokalamu. |
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-portable.zip` | **Hāpai lima (Portable).** Wehe i ka zip ma kēlā me kēia wahi a holo iā `decoy.exe`. Noho nā ʻikepili a pau ma ka waihona `data` e pili ana, ʻaʻohe mea e kākau ʻia i kāu ʻikepili mea hoʻohana. Holoi i ka waihona e wehe loa ai. |

E hōʻoia i kekahi hoʻoiho me `SHA256SUMS.txt` (`Get-FileHash <file>` ma PowerShell).

Hiki i Windows SmartScreen ke aʻo aku e pili ana i kekahi mea hoʻopuka i ʻike ʻole ʻia no ka mea ʻaʻole i pūlima ʻia nā haku me ke code signing; koho i *Nā ʻike hou aʻe → Holo nō naʻe* inā hilinaʻi ʻoe i ka haku.

Nā Koina: Windows 10 a i ʻole 11 (64-bit) me ka WebView2 runtime (ua hoʻokomo mua ʻia ma Windows 11 a me nā hoʻoponopono hou o Windows 10).

## Hoʻohana

1. E wehe i ka **Waihona pāʻani** a huli i kekahi pāʻani, a i ʻole kāomi iā `Ctrl` + `K` mai kēlā me kēia wahi.
2. Kāomi i kekahi pāʻani. Ma ka papa ma ka ʻaoʻao ʻākau, koho i ka polokalamu a me nā minuke e mālama ai (pono pinepine nā hana Discord i 15).
3. Kāomi iā **Hoʻomaka i ka decoy**. E hōʻike ʻo Discord i ka pāʻani ma hope o kekahi mau kekona.
4. E nānā ma lalo o **Ke holo nei**, hoʻopau i hoʻokahi a i nā mea a pau i kēlā me kēia manawa, a i ʻole e waiho wale iā Decoy ma ka pā ʻōnaehana.

Hūnā maʻamau ʻia nā pāʻani me ka ʻole o nā polokalamu hoʻokō ma ko Discord papa inoa (ʻaʻole hiki ke ʻike ʻia lākou). E hoʻā i ka *Hōʻike i nā pāʻani me ka ʻole o ka exe* e ʻike ai iā lākou; hoʻoponopono hou ʻia ka papa inoa i kēlā me kēia lā, no laila e mākaukau koke lākou ke hoʻohui ʻo Discord i kekahi polokalamu.

### Laina Kauoha

```
decoy.exe --launch "<kahi o ka polokalamu mai ka papa inoa Discord>" [minuke]
```

Hoʻomaka koke i kekahi decoy, no ka laʻana `decoy.exe --launch "robloxplayerbeta.exe" 15`.

## Haku mai ka molekumu (source)

Nā mea e pono ai: [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 20+, ka pono paʻa o [Rust](https://rustup.rs) (MSVC target) me nā mea hana haku Visual Studio C++, a me ka WebView2 runtime.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/leon03131/Decoy.git
cd Decoy
npm install

npm run tauri dev      # holo ma ke kūlana hoʻomohala me ke kākoʻo hou koke (hot reload)
npm run tauri build    # haku i ka mea hoʻonoho ma src-tauri/target/release/bundle/nsis
```

Nā hōʻoia maikaʻi:

```bash
npm run check-locales  # hōʻoia i kēlā me kēia waihona unuhi e pili ana iā ru.json
npx tsc --noEmit       # nānā i nā ʻano kikokiko ma ka frontend
cargo check --manifest-path src-tauri/Cargo.toml
```

Nā Manaʻo: Hoʻohui ʻia ka exe decoy e `src-tauri/build.rs` me `rustc` maʻamau mai `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs` a laila hoʻokomo ʻia i loko o ka polokalamu. Ke holo ka frontend ma kekahi polokalamu kele pūnaewele maʻamau (`npm run dev`), hoʻohana ʻia kekahi backend hoʻopunipuni liʻiliʻi (`src/mock.ts`), he mea pono no ka hana ʻana ma ka UI.

## Nā Unuhi

Aia nā huaʻōlelo UI ma [`src/locales`](../../src/locales), hoʻokahi waihona JSON no kēlā me kēia ʻōlelo (`en.json`, `ru.json`, ...).
ʻO ka hoʻohui ʻana i kekahi ʻōlelo, ʻo ia ka hoʻohui ʻana i kekahi waihona; ʻaʻohe loli o ke code. E nānā iā [docs/TRANSLATING.md](../TRANSLATING.md) no ke ʻano o ka waihona, kekahi kumu noi mākaukau no ka unuhi mīkini a me ka palapala hōʻoia. Hele nā unuhi o kēia README i `docs/i18n/README.<code>.md`.

## Ka Papa Papahana

```
src/                    React + TypeScript frontend
  components/           Waihona pāʻani, papa kikoʻī, nā makemake, ...
  locales/              nā waihona unuhi (hoʻokahi JSON no kēlā me kēia ʻōlelo)
  i18n-core.ts          hoʻouka ʻōlelo a huli
  store.ts              kūlana o ka polokalamu (zustand)
src-tauri/              Rust backend (Tauri 2)
  src/lib.rs            hōkeo ʻikepili pāʻani, nā polokalamu decoy, pā ʻōnaehana
  stub/stub.rs          ka polokalamu pani e hoʻokomo ʻia
scripts/                nā palapala kōkua (hōʻoia i nā ʻōlelo)
docs/                   alakaʻi unuhi, nā kiʻi paʻi papakaumaka
```

## Kūpono pilikino

- Hoʻohana wale ʻo Decoy i ka pūnaewele no ka hoʻoiho ʻana i kā Discord papa inoa huli lehulehu (ma ka hoʻomaka mua, a laila ma kahi o hoʻokahi manawa i ka lā a i ʻole ke kāomi ʻoe i ka hoʻoponopono hou) a, no ka hōʻike ʻana, nā kiʻi liʻiliʻi a me nā hana noʻeau o nā pāʻani mai ka CDN lehulehu o Discord.
- ʻAʻole ia e heluhelu, mālama a hoʻouna paha i kāu token Discord a i ʻole kekahi ʻikepili moʻokāki, a ʻaʻole ia e kamaʻilio me ka polokalamu Discord.
- Mālama ʻia nā makemake (nā punahele, ʻōlelo, nā manawa) ma kahi kokoke i loko o ka waihona WebView o ka polokalamu; noho ka hūnā ʻikepili o ka papa inoa pāʻani a me nā waihona decoy noho pōkole ma `%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.decoy.desktop`.
- ʻAʻohe ʻikepili kālailai (analytics), ʻaʻohe hōʻike hāʻule, ʻaʻohe waihona moʻokāki.

## Nā Palena i ʻIke ʻia

- Hiki iā Discord ke hoʻololi i ke ʻano o ka huli ʻana i kēlā me kēia manawa. Inā pau ka ʻike ʻia ʻana o kekahi decoy, e ʻoluʻolu e wehe i kekahi issue.
- Pono kekahi mau hana i nā mea ʻoi aku ma mua o kekahi polokalamu e holo wale nei (no ka laʻana ke noho ʻana i loko o kekahi ʻike kūikawā o kekahi kahua pāʻani). Hoʻohālike wale nō ʻo Decoy i ka polokalamu.
- ʻAʻole hiki ke ʻike ʻia nā helu me ka ʻole o nā polokalamu hoʻokō ma ka papa inoa o Discord.
- I kekahi manawa, e aʻo aku nā polokalamu palekana e pili ana i nā polokalamu hoʻokō liʻiliʻi i pūlima ʻole ʻia e noho mālie ana ma ka hoʻomanaʻo (memory). He kumu wehe ka decoy a ua haku ʻia mai `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs`; hiki iā ʻoe ke hōʻuluʻulu ponoʻī iā ia.

## Hoʻokuʻu kuleana

Hāʻawi ʻia ʻo Decoy ma ke ʻano "e like me ia" no nā hana pilikino, hoʻonaʻauao a me ka hoʻāʻo ʻana. Hiki i ka hoʻohālike ʻana i kekahi pāʻani e holo nei ke hōʻeha i nā Kūlana Hana o Discord a i ʻole o ka mea hoʻopuka pāʻani, a hiki ke alakaʻi i kekahi hana kūʻē i kāu moʻokāki. ʻO ʻoe wale nō ke kuleana no kāu hoʻohana ʻana iā ia. ʻO "Discord" a me nā inoa pāʻani a pau a me nā kiʻi he mau hōʻailona kālepa ia o ko lākou mau mea nona ponoʻī a hoʻohana wale ʻia ma ʻaneʻi e hoʻomaopopo i ka polokalamu a lākou e kuhikuhi nei.
