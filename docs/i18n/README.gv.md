# Decoy

**Obbragheyyn beggey aundyrys ta cur er Discord smooinaghtyn dy vel gamman goll.**

![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Windows%2010%20%7C%2011-7c6cf6)
![Built with](https://img.shields.io/badge/built%20with-Tauri%202%20%2B%20Rust-7c6cf6)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-7c6cf6)

<img src="../screenshots/library.jpg" width="860" alt="Lioarlan Decoy lesh gamanyn er y gherrid as ellyn" />

</div>

Ta Decoy freill obbraghey aundyrys neu-vegganagh lesh uinnag fo ennym executable y ghamman, myr shen ta cronnaghey gamanyn Discord recortys dy vel oo cloie yn gamman shen. Cha nel feme ayd er y feer ghamman y chur er bun ny y stoyral erbee, myr shen cha nel red mooar erbee er dty zisk.

Ta fys echey er **dagh ooilley gamman nod Discord cronnaghey** (mysh 25,000 entryn) er-yn-oyr dy vel eh lhaih rolley cronnaghey theayagh Discord hene as freill eh noa liorish hene.

> [!WARNING]
> Foddys jannoo brynneraghey gamman goll noi Conaantyn Shirveish Discord as t'ou jannoo ymmyd jeh Decoy **er dty gaue hene**.
> Cha nel kiangley, coardailys ny bentynys erbee ec y chlaare shoh rish Discord Inc. Jeeagh er [Yiooldey](#yiooldey).

## Troyn

- **Dagh nhee nod Discord cronnaghey.** Ta rolley ny gamanyn er ny laadey sheese veih bun-phoynt cronnaghey theayagh Discord, stoyrit dy ynnydagh as noait gagh laa. Ta gamanyn noa as executables noa ta currit rish ry-akin liorish hene.
- **Shirrey ta tannaghtyn seose.** Shirrey tappee rere ennym y ghamman ny executable trooid thousaneyn dy ghamanyn, reayrt leacagyn ny rolley coon, reihyn as straih “Er y gherrid”.
- **Un chleeg.** Reih gamman, reih yn executable (my ta ny smoo na fer ayn), reih kead cha liauyr dy reayll eh as broo toshiaght. Ta fainey earroo sheese as fogrey ginsh dhyt tra ta'n traa seose.
- **Cha nel eh faagail red erbee ny yei.** Cha nel decoy ayn agh tra t'eh goll: ta'n exe beg goll er croo ec y toshiaght as dollit magh ec y jerrey. Ta dagh nhee goll er scuirr tra t'ou faagail, as ta fuilleeaghyn veih tuittym goll er glaney ec y chied chur er bun elley.
- **Kiadagh.** T'eh cummal ayns treiy y cho-earrooder, gyn uinnagyn ny crammanyn barra-obbree da ny decoyn hene.
- **Çhyndayssyn foshlit.** Ta'n eddyr-eaghtyr goll er stiurey liorish coadanyn cadjin JSON, myr shen cha nel ayns cur çhengey rish agh un choadan. Ta Baarle as Rooshish goaill stiagh.
- **Preevaadjagh.** Gyn coontys, gyn token Discord, gyn tele-veadraght. Jeeagh er [Preevaadjys](#preevaadjys).

## Kish myr t'eh gobbraghey

1. Ta Discord briwnys dy vel oo cloie gamman liorish jeeaghyn er ny obbragheyyn ta goll er dty cho-earrooder as cosoylaghey nyn enmyn executable rish rolley theayagh (`/api/v9/applications/detectable`).
2. Ta Decoy laadey sheese y rolley shen as, son y ghamman t'ou reih, t'eh jeant coip jeh claare aundyrys beg (mysh 120 KB, screeut ayns Rust as currit stiagh ayns Decoy) dys coodagh shallidagh lesh yn ennym executable veih'n rolley, myr sampleyr `.../cod26-cod.exe`.
3. Cha nel y claare aundyrys jannoo red erbee agh gymmyrkey un uinnag ard-cheim, soit foddey magh ass y scailley nagh vaik oo rieau eh. Cha nel Discord cur enney agh er obbraghey lesh uinnag, shen y fa nagh vel obbraghey gyn uinnag goll er cronnaghey erbee.
4. Çheusthie jeh mysh 15 shallidyn ta Discord taishbyney yn gamman myr dty obbyr reirey.
5. Tra ta'n traa-reilleyder kioneaghey ny tra t'ou broo stad, ta Decoy cur jerrey er yn obbraghey as dolley magh y coadan. Ta nhee obbyr Windows shickyraghey dy vel decoyn baase marish Decoy, ga dy jinnagh eh tuittym.

## Cur er bun

Reih fer veih dullag ny [Lhiggydyn Magh](../../../../releases):

| Laadey Sheese | Son |
|---|---|
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` | **Currer er bun.** Rere yn ymmydeyr, gyn feme er kiartyn reireyder, cur stiagh entry roie-rollagh Toshiaght as neu-hroggalagh. |
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-portable.zip` | **Ymmyrkagh.** Jee-phackey ayns boayl erbee as roie `decoy.exe`. Ta dagh fysseree tannaghtyn 'sy choodagh `data` liorish, cha nel red erbee screeut dys dty choontys ymmydeyr. Doll magh y coodagh dy scughey eh. |

Shickyree laadey sheese lesh `SHA256SUMS.txt` (`Get-FileHash <coadan>` ayns PowerShell).

Foddys Windows SmartScreen cur raaue bentyn rish soilshagher neu-enmyssit er-yn-oyr nagh vel ny troggalyn fo-screeut lesh coad; reih *Tuilleadh fysseree → Roie mar shen* my ta treisht ayd 'sy troggal.

Feme er: Windows 10 ny 11 (64-bit) lesh yn amm-roie WebView2 (goaill stiagh hannah ayns Windows 11 as noaghyn noa jeh Windows 10).

## Ymmyd

1. Foshil **Lioarlan** as shir son gamman, ny broo `Ctrl` + `K` veih boayl erbee.
2. Lhieg er gamman. 'Sy phannel er y laue yesh reih yn obbraghey as kead dy vinnidyn dy reayll eh (ta jannoo Discord shirrey 15 dy cadjin).
3. Broo **Cur er bun decoy**. Bee Discord taishbyney yn gamman lurg kuse dy shallidyn.
4. Jeeagh er fo **Goll**, stad fer ny dagh ooilley fer ec amm erbee, ny faag Decoy 'sy treiy.

Ta gamanyn nagh vel executables ec nyn n-entry Discord folliht dy cadjin (cha nod ad ve er nyn gronnaghey). Lhig da *Taishbynee gamanyn gyn exe* dy vakin ad; ta'n rolley noait gagh laa, myr shen t'ad ry-gheddyn chomh luath as ta Discord cur obbraghey rish.

### Linney-sarree

```
decoy.exe --launch "<cass executable veih rolley Discord>" [minnidyn]
```

Cur er bun decoy chelleeragh, myr sampleyr `decoy.exe --launch "robloxplayerbeta.exe" 15`.

## Troggal veih bun-chooish

Reo-feme: [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 20+, yn ghreieys shassooagh [Rust](https://rustup.rs) (MSVC target) marish greienyn troggal Visual Studio C++, as yn amm-roie WebView2.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/leon03131/Decoy.git
cd Decoy
npm install

npm run tauri dev      # roie ayns mod lhiassaghey lesh aalaadey bio
npm run tauri build    # trog yn currer er bun ayns src-tauri/target/release/bundle/nsis
```

Shickyraghey ymmydoil:

```bash
npm run check-locales  # shickyree dagh coadan çhyndays noi ru.json
npx tsc --noEmit       # seentaghid y toshiaght
cargo check --manifest-path src-tauri/Cargo.toml
```

Noteyn: ta'n decoy exe goll er chroo liorish `src-tauri/build.rs` marish `rustc` cadjin veih `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs` as lurg shen currit stiagh 'sy chlaare. Tra ta'n toshiaght roie ayns jeeagheyder cadjin (`npm run dev`) ta cooylrey crouwagh beg (`src/mock.ts`) goll er ymmyd, ta feer lajer son obbraghey er yn UI.

## Çhyndayssyn

Ta teudyn UI cummal ayns [`src/locales`](../../src/locales), un choadan JSON son dagh çhengey (`en.json`, `ru.json`, ...).
Cur çhengey rish shen cur coadan rish; gyn caghlaa coad. Jeeagh er [docs/TRANSLATING.md](../TRANSLATING.md) son cummey y choadan, prompt aarloo son jannoo ymmyd jeh çhyndeyder jeshaght as y script shickyraghey. Ta çhyndayssyn jeh'n README shoh goll dys `docs/i18n/README.<code>.md`.

## Kiarail y chlaare

```
src/                    React + TypeScript toshiaght
  components/           Lioarlan, pannel myn-phoynt, reaghaghyn, ...
  locales/              coadanyn çhyndays (un JSON gagh çhengey)
  i18n-core.ts          laadey çhengey as shirrey
  store.ts              stayd y chlaare (zustand)
src-tauri/              Rust cooylrey (Tauri 2)
  src/lib.rs            bun-fysseree gamanyn, obbragheyyn decoy, treiy
  stub/stub.rs          y claare aundyrys ta currit stiagh
scripts/                scriptyn cooney (shickyraghey ynnydagh)
docs/                   stiurey çhyndays, graafanyn-scailley
```

## Preevaadjys

- Cha nel Decoy jannoo ymmyd jeh'n eddyr-voggyl agh son laadey sheese rolley cronnaghey theayagh Discord (er y chied toshiaght, as lurg shen cha smoo na un cheayrt 'sy laa ny tra t'ou broo noaee) as, son taishbyney, cowraghyn gamman as ellyn veih CDN theayagh Discord.
- Cha nel eh lhaih, stoyral ny cur dty hoken Discord ny fysseree coontys erbee, as cha nel eh loayrt rish y chliant Discord.
- Ta reaghaghyn (reihyn, çhengey, faiddyn) er nyn stoyral dy ynnydagh ayns stoyr WebView y chlaare; ta tashtey rolley ny gamanyn as coadanyn shallidagh decoy cummal ayns `%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.decoy.desktop`.
- Gyn bun-earrooaght, gyn fys er tuittym, gyn coontyssyn.

## Cagliaghyn er fys

- Foddys Discord caghlaa kish myr ta cronnaghey gobbraghey ec amm erbee. My ta decoy scuirr ve er ny chronnaghey, foshil cooish leih.
- Ta feme ec kuse dy ghleashaghtyn er ny smoo na obbraghey ta goll (myr sampleyr ve sthie ayns traa-ennaghtyn er lheh er ardán gamman). Cha nel Decoy agh arrish yn obbraghey.
- Cha nod entryn gyn executables ayns rolley Discord ve er nyn gronnaghey.
- Ny keayrtyn ta coad-voggyl sauçhys cur cowrey er executables beggey neu-heynit ta soie ayns cooinaghtyn. Ta'n decoy ny vunn-foshlit as troggit veih `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs`; foddys oo eh y hroggal hene.

## Yiooldey

Ta Decoy er ny chiarail "myr t'eh" son cooishyn persoonagh, ynseydagh as prowaltys. Foddys arrish gamman ta goll ve noi Conaantyn Shirveish Discord ny soilsheyder y ghamman, as foddys eh cur er dty choontys ve gortit. T'ou hene freggyrtagh er kish myr t'ou jannoo ymmyd jeh. Ta "Discord" as dagh ennym gamman as cowrey nyn dreid-chowraghyn ec nyn seyrnyn hene as cha nel ad goll er ymmyd ayns shoh agh dy chowraghey yn coad-voggyl t'ad bentyn rish.
