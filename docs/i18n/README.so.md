# Decoy

**Habsocodyo yar yar oo meel-ku-gaar ah oo Discord ka dhigaya inuu u maleeyo in ciyaar ay socoto.**

![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Windows%2010%20%7C%2011-7c6cf6)
![Built with](https://img.shields.io/badge/built%20with-Tauri%202%20%2B%20Rust-7c6cf6)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-7c6cf6)

<img src="../screenshots/library.jpg" width="860" alt="Maktabadda Decoy oo ay ku jiraan ciyaarihii ugu dambeeyay iyo sawirrada farshaxanka" />

</div>

Decoy waxay haysaa habsocod meel-ku-gaar ah oo aan waxyeello lahayn, daaqadna leh oo ku hoos shaqeynaya magaca faylka exe ee ciyaarta, si ogaanshaha ciyaarta ee Discord uu kuugu muujiyo inaad ciyaartaas ciyaareyso. Weligaa uma baahnid inaad rakibto ama furto ciyaarta dhabta ah, sidaas darteed wax culus kuma kaydsanaadaan diskigaaga.

Waxay taqaanaa **ciyaar kasta oo Discord uu ogaan karo** (qiyaastii 25,000 oo xogood) sababtoo ah waxay akhrisaa liiska guud ee ogaanshaha ee Discord laftiisa oo si toos ah ayay u cusbooneysiisaa.

> [!WARNING]
> Iska yeelyeelka ciyaar socota waxay ka horimaan kartaa Shuruudaha Adeegga ee Discord waxaadna Decoy ku isticmaaleysaa **khatartaada gaarka ah**.
> Mashruucani kuma xirna, laguma taageerin mana khuseeyo Discord Inc. Eeg [Afeef](#afeef).

## Astaamaha

- **Wax kasta oo Discord uu ogaan karo.** Liiska ciyaaraha waxaa laga soo dejiyaa xiriiriyaha guud ee ogaanshaha ee Discord, waxaana lagu keydiyaa gudaha iyadoo maalin kasta la cusbooneysiinayo. Ciyaaraha cusub iyo faylasha exe ee dhowaan lagu daray waxay u soo baxaan si toos ah.
- **Raadin xawaare ku socota.** Raadin degdeg ah oo ku saleysan magaca ciyaarta ama exe ee kumanaan ciyaarood ah, qaabka qaybaha ama liis kooban, kuwa la jeclaaday iyo safka "Dhawaan".
- **Hal gujin.** Dooro ciyaar, dooro faylka exe (haddii ay dhowr yihiin), dooro inta daqiiqo ee la haynayo oo guji bilaab. Wareeg cabbirka waqtiga iyo ogeysiis ayaa kuu sheegaya marka waqtigu dhaco.
- **Wax dambe kama tago.** Decoy wuxuu jiraa oo keliya inta uu shaqeynayo: faylka yar ee exe waxaa la abuuraa marka la bilaabo waxaana la tirtiraa marka la joojiyo. Wax walba waa la joojiyaa marka aad ka baxdo, haddii uu barnaamijku si lama filaan ah u xirmana hadhaaga waxaa la nadiifiyaa marka xigta ee la furo.
- **Aamusan.** Wuxuu joogaa saxaaradda nidaamka (system tray), ma laha daaqado ama badhammo ku yaal barta howlaha (taskbar) ee decoy-yada laftooda.
- **Turjumaado furan.** Muuqaalka barnaamijka waxaa hagaya faylal JSON oo fudud, sidaas darteed ku darista luuqad cusub waa hal fayl oo keliya. Ingiriis iyo Ruush ayaa ku jira.
- **Xog dhowr.** Xisaab ma laha, astaan Discord (token) ma leh, macluumaad qarsoodi ah oo la diro ma jiraan (telemetry). Eeg [Qarsoodiga](#qarsoodiga).

## Sida ay u shaqeyso

1. Discord wuxuu go'aansadaa inaad ciyaar ciyaareyso markuu eego habsocodyada ka shaqeynaya kombiyuutarkaaga oo uu magacyadooda exe barbar dhigo liiska guud (`/api/v9/applications/detectable`).
2. Decoy wuxuu soo dejiyaa liiskaas, ciyaarta aad dooratana, wuxuu nuqul ka mid ah barnaamij yar oo meel-ku-gaar ah (qiyaastii 120 KB, oo ku qoran Rust kuna dhex jira Decoy) ku koobiyeeyaa gal kumeelgaar ah isagoo isticmaalaya magaca exe ee liiska ku jira, tusaale ahaan `.../cod26-cod.exe`.
3. Barnaamijka meel-ku-gaarka ah waxba ma sameeyo marka laga reebo lahaanshaha hal daaqad sare oo fog oo ka baxsan shaashadda si aadan marnaba u arag. Discord wuxuu ogaadaa oo keliya habsocod daaqad leh, waana sababta habsocodka aan daaqadda lahayn gebi ahaanba loo iska indhatiro.
4. Qiyaastii 15 ilbiriqsi gudahood Discord wuxuu ciyaarta ku muujinayaa waxqabadkaaga hadda.
5. Marka waqtigu dhaco ama aad gujiso jooji, Decoy wuxuu soo afjaraa habsocodka wuxuuna tirtiraa faylka. Shayga shaqada ee Windows (Windows job object) wuxuu hubiyaa in decoy-yadu la dhintaan Decoy, xitaa haddii uu barnaamijku xirmo.

## Rakibidda

Ka dooro mid bogga [Wararka la sii daayay](../../../../releases):

| Soo dejiso | Ku habboon |
|---|---|
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` | **Rakibe (Installer).** Isticmaale kasta, uma baahna mudnaan maamule, wuxuu ku darayaa liiska Start menu iyo barnaamijka tirtiraha. |
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-portable.zip` | **La qaadi karo (Portable).** Ku furi meel kasta oo socodsii `decoy.exe`. Dhammaan xogtu waxay ku jirtaa galka `data` ee ku xiga, waxba laguma qoro xogta isticmaalahaaga. Tirtir galka si aad uga saarto. |

Xaqiiji soo dejinta adigoo isticmaalaya `SHA256SUMS.txt` (`Get-FileHash <faylka>` gudaha PowerShell).

Windows SmartScreen ayaa laga yaabaa inuu ka digtoonaado daabacaha aan la aqoonsanayn sababtoo ah faylashu ma laha saxiixa koodka; dooro *Faahfaahin dheeraad ah → Baro si kasta* haddii aad aaminsan tahay faylka.

Shuruudaha: Windows 10 ama 11 (64-bit) oo leh WebView2 runtime (mar horeba ku dhex jira Windows 11 iyo cusbooneysiisyadii ugu dambeeyay ee Windows 10).

## Isticmaalka

1. Fur **Maktabadda** oo raadi ciyaar, ama riix `Ctrl` + `K` meel kasta.
2. Guji ciyaar. Qeybta dhanka midig ka dooro habsocodka iyo inta daqiiqo ee la haynayo (howlaha Discord inta badan waxay codsadaan 15).
3. Guji **Bilaab decoy**. Discord wuxuu muujin doonaa ciyaarta dhowr ilbiriqsi ka dib.
4. Kala soco qaybta **Shaqeynaya**, jooji mid ama dhammaan wakhti kasta, ama ku daa Decoy saxaaradda nidaamka.

Ciyaaraha aysan Discord wali ku darin faylasha exe si toos ah ayaa loo qariyaa (lama ogaan karo). Shid *Muuji ciyaaraha aan exe lahayn* si aad u aragto; liiska maalin kasta ayaa la cusbooneysiiyaa, sidaas darteed waxay noqdaan kuwo diyaar ah isla marka uu Discord ku daro habsocod.

### Khadka amarka (Command line)

```
decoy.exe --launch "<waddada executable-ka ee liiska Discord>" [daqiiqo]
```

Wuxuu bilaabayaa decoy isla markiiba, tusaale ahaan `decoy.exe --launch "robloxplayerbeta.exe" 15`.

## Ka dhis koodka asalka ah (Build from source)

Shuruudaha lagama maarmaanka ah: [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 20+, aaladaha deggan ee [Rust](https://rustup.rs) (ujeeddada MSVC) oo ay la socdaan qalabka dhismaha ee Visual Studio C++, iyo WebView2 runtime.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/leon03131/Decoy.git
cd Decoy
npm install

npm run tauri dev      # ku socodsii qaabka horumarinta adoo adeegsanaya cusbooneysiin toos ah
npm run tauri build    # soo saar faylka rakibaha ee src-tauri/target/release/bundle/nsis
```

Hubino waxtar leh:

```bash
npm run check-locales  # ku xaqiiji fayl kasta oo turjumaad ah ru.json
npx tsc --noEmit       # baadh noocyada koodka hore (frontend)
cargo check --manifest-path src-tauri/Cargo.toml
```

Xusuusin: exe-ka decoy waxaa lagu dhisay `src-tauri/build.rs` iyadoo la adeegsanayo `rustc` caadi ah oo ka socda `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs` ka dibna lagu dhex daray barnaamijka. Marka qaybta hore ay ku shaqeyso biraawsar caadi ah (`npm run dev`) waxaa la adeegsadaa backend yar oo tijaabo ah (`src/mock.ts`), taas oo ku habboon la shaqeynta UI.

## Turjumaadaha

Qoraallada UI waxay ku jiraan [`src/locales`](../../src/locales), hal fayl oo JSON ah luuqad kasta (`en.json`, `ru.json`, ...). Ku darista luuqad waxay la macno tahay ku darista fayl; ma jiraan wax kood ah oo la beddelayo. Eeg [docs/TRANSLATING.md](../TRANSLATING.md) si aad u hesho qaabka faylka, hage diyaarsan oo loogu talagalay turjumaada mishiinka iyo qoraalka xaqiijinta. Turjumaada README-gan waxay tagtaa `docs/i18n/README.<code>.md`.

## Qaab-dhismeedka mashruuca

```
src/                    React + TypeScript frontend
  components/           Library, detail panel, settings, ...
  locales/              translation files (one JSON per language)
  i18n-core.ts          language loading and lookup
  store.ts              application state (zustand)
src-tauri/              Rust backend (Tauri 2)
  src/lib.rs            game database, decoy processes, tray
  stub/stub.rs          the stand-in program that gets embedded
scripts/                helper scripts (locale validation)
docs/                   translating guide, screenshots
```

## Qarsoodiga

- Decoy wuxuu kaliya u isticmaalaa internetka si uu u soo dejiyo liiska guud ee ogaanshaha ee Discord (marka ugu horeysa ee la bilaabo, ka dibna ugu badnaan hal mar maalintii ama marka aad riixdo cusbooneysii) iyo, soo bandhigista astaamaha ciyaaraha iyo farshaxanka laga helo CDN-ka guud ee Discord.
- Ma akhriyo, ma kaydiyo mana diro astaantaada Discord ama wax xog ah oo ku saabsan koontadaada, lamana hadlo barnaamijka Discord.
- Dejinta (kuwa la jeclaaday, luuqadda, waqtiyada) waxaa lagu kaydiyaa gudaha kaydka WebView ee barnaamijka; kaydka liiska ciyaaraha iyo faylasha kumeelgaarka ah ee decoy waxay galaan `%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.decoy.desktop`.
- Ma jiraan tirakoobyo (analytics), ma jiraan warbixino cilladeed, mana jiraan xisaabaadyo.

## Xaddidaadaha la yaqaan

- Discord wuxuu beddeli karaa sida ogaanshaha u shaqeeyo wakhti kasta. Haddii decoy la joojiyo ogaanshihiisa, fadlan fur warbixin (issue).
- Waxqabadyada qaarkood waxay u baahan yihiin wax ka badan habsocod socda oo keliya (tusaale ahaan joogitaanka khibrad gaar ah oo ku saabsan goobta ciyaarta). Decoy wuxuu ku daydaa habsocodka oo keliya.
- Xogaha aan lahayn faylasha exe ee liiska Discord lama ogaan karo.
- Barnaamijyada amniga mararka qaarkood waxay calaamadeeyaan faylasha exe ee yaryar ee aan saxiixnayn ee aan waxba qabanayn oo xusuusta ku jira. Decoy waa il furan waxaana laga dhisay `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs`; waad iskii u dhisi kartaa naftaada.

## Afeef

Decoy waxaa loo bixiyay "sida uu yahay" ujeeddooyin shaqsiyeed, waxbarasho iyo tijaabo ah. Iska yeelyeelka ciyaar socota waxay jebin kartaa Shuruudaha Adeegga ee Discord ama kuwa daabacaha ciyaarta, waxayna keeni kartaa in tallaabo laga qaado akoonkaaga. Adiga kaliya ayaa mas'uul ka ah sida aad u isticmaasho. "Discord" iyo dhammaan magacyada ciyaaraha iyo astaamahoodu waa calaamado ganacsi oo ay leeyihiin milkiilayaashooda waxaana halkan loogu isticmaalaa oo keliya in lagu garto softiweerka ay tilmaamayaan.
