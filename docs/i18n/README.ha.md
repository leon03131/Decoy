# Decoy

**Ƙananan hanyoyin maye gurbi da ke sa Discord tunanin cewa wasa na kunne.**

![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Windows%2010%20%7C%2011-7c6cf6)
![Built with](https://img.shields.io/badge/built%20with-Tauri%202%20%2B%20Rust-7c6cf6)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-7c6cf6)

<img src="../screenshots/library.jpg" width="860" alt="Dakin karatun Decoy mai dauke da wasannin baya-bayan nan da zane-zane" />

</div>

Decoy yana sanya wani shiri na maye gurbi mara lahani mai taga yana aiki a ƙarƙashin sunan fayil ɗin wasa mai aiwatarwa (executable), don haka gano wasa na Discord zai nuna kana buga wannan wasan. Ba kwa buƙatar shigarwa ko kunna ainihin wasan, don haka babu wani babban abu da zai zauna a rumbun kwamfutarka.

Yana sane da **kowane wasa da Discord zai iya ganowa** (kusan abubuwa 25,000) saboda yana karanta jerin abubuwan ganowa na fili na Discord kuma yana sabunta shi da kansa.

> [!WARNING]
> Yin bogi game da wasan da ke aiki na iya saɓa wa Sharuɗɗan Sabis na Discord kuma kuna amfani da Decoy **a kan haɗarinku**.
> Wannan aikin ba shi da alaƙa da, ba a amince da shi ta, ko haɗa shi da Discord Inc. Duba [Gargaɗi](#gargaɗi).

## Siffofi

- **Duk abin da Discord zai iya ganowa.** Ana saukar da jerin wasannin daga hanyar gano jama'a ta Discord, an ajiye shi a cikin na'ura kuma ana sabunta shi kowace rana. Sabbin wasanni da sabbin fayiloli masu aiwatarwa da aka ƙara suna bayyana da kansu.
- **Bincike mai sauri.** Bincike nan take ta sunan wasa ko mai aiwatarwa a cikin dubban wasanni, duba mai grid ko ƙaramin jeri, abubuwan da aka fi so da layin "Na baya-bayan nan".
- **Danna sau ɗaya.** Zaɓi wasa, zaɓi mai aiwatarwa (idan akwai da yawa), zaɓi tsawon lokacin da za a riƙe shi kuma danna fara. Zoben ƙidayar lokaci da sanarwa za su gaya muku lokacin da lokaci ya ƙare.
- **Baya barin komai a baya.** Decoy yana wanzuwa ne kawai yayin da yake aiki: ana ƙirƙirar ƙaramar exe lokacin farawa kuma a share ta yayin tsayawa. Ana dakatar da komai lokacin da kuka fita, kuma ana share ragowar haɗari a lokacin ƙaddamarwa ta gaba.
- **Mai natsuwa.** Yana zaune a cikin tire ɗin tsarin (system tray), babu tagogi ko maɓallan taskbar don decoys da kansu.
- **Fassarori a buɗe.** Tsarin yana aiki ne ta hanyar fayilolin JSON na yau da kullun, don haka ƙara yare fayil ɗaya ne kawai. An haɗa da Turanci da Rashanci.
- **Sirri.** Babu asusu, babu token na Discord, babu telemetry. Duba [Sirri](#sirri).

## Yadda yake aiki

1. Discord yana yanke shawarar cewa kana buga wasa ta hanyar duba hanyoyin (processes) da ke gudana a kan PC ɗinka da kuma kwatanta sunayensu masu aiwatarwa da jerin jama'a (`/api/v9/applications/detectable`).
2. Decoy yana saukar da wannan jerin kuma, ga wasan da kuka zaɓa, yana kwafi ƙaramin shirin maye gurbi (kusan KB 120, wanda aka rubuta a Rust kuma aka saka a cikin Decoy) zuwa babban fayil na wucin gadi ta amfani da sunan mai aiwatarwa daga jerin, misali `.../cod26-cod.exe`.
3. Shirin maye gurbi ba ya yin komai sai dai mallakar taga ɗaya na babban matakin, wanda aka ajiye shi da nisa daga allon don ba za ku taɓa ganinsa ba. Discord yana gane tsari ne kawai wanda ya mallaki taga, wanda shine dalilin da ya sa aka yi watsi da tsarin da ba shi da taga kwata-kwata.
4. A cikin kusan daƙiƙa 15 Discord yana nuna wasan a matsayin ayyukanku na yanzu.
5. Lokacin da mai ƙidayar lokaci ya ƙare ko kuka danna tsayawa, Decoy yana ƙare tsarin kuma ya share fayil ɗin. Wani abu na aikin Windows (Windows job object) yana tabbatar da cewa decoys sun mutu tare da Decoy, koda kuwa ya faɗi (crash).

## Shigarwa

Zaɓi ɗaya daga shafin [Releases](../../../../releases):

| Saukewa | Domin |
|---|---|
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` | **Mai sakawa.** Ga kowane mai amfani, ba a buƙatar haƙƙin mai gudanarwa (admin), yana ƙara shigarwar menu na Fara da mai cirewa. |
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-portable.zip` | **Mai ɗaukuwa.** Bude zip a ko'ina kuma gudanar da `decoy.exe`. Duk bayanai suna zama a cikin babban fayil ɗin `data` kusa da shi, ba a rubuta komai a bayanan martaba na mai amfani ba. Share babban fayil ɗin don cire shi. |

Tabbatar da saukewa tare da `SHA256SUMS.txt` (`Get-FileHash <file>` a PowerShell).

Windows SmartScreen na iya yin gargaɗi game da mawallafin da ba a sani ba saboda ba a sanya hannun lambar gini ba; zaɓi *Ƙarin bayani → Gudanar da ko ta yaya* idan kun amince da ginin.

Abubuwan da ake buƙata: Windows 10 ko 11 (64-bit) tare da lokacin aiki na WebView2 (an riga an haɗa shi a cikin Windows 11 da sabuntawar Windows 10 na baya-bayan nan).

## Amfani

1. Buɗe **Dakin karatu** kuma bincika wasa, ko danna `Ctrl` + `K` daga ko'ina.
2. Danna wasa. A cikin allon da ke hannun dama zaɓi tsarin da mintuna nawa za a riƙe shi (ayyukan Discord yawanci suna neman 15).
3. Danna **Fara decoy**. Discord zai nuna wasan bayan ƴan daƙiƙa.
4. Kalli shi a ƙarƙashin **Mai gudana**, dakatar da ɗaya ko duka a kowane lokaci, ko kawai bar Decoy a cikin tire.

Wasannin da shigarwarsu ta Discord ba ta da fayiloli masu aiwatarwa har yanzu an ɓoye su ta tsohuwa (ba za a iya gano su ba). Kunna *Nuna wasanni marasa exe* don ganin su; ana sabunta jerin kowace rana, don haka suna samuwa da zaran Discord ya ƙara tsari.

### Layin umarni

```
decoy.exe --launch "<executable path from the Discord list>" [minutes]
```

Yana fara decoy nan da nan, misali `decoy.exe --launch "robloxplayerbeta.exe" 15`.

## Gina daga tushe

Abubuwan da ake buƙata: [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 20+, tsayayyen kayan aiki na [Rust](https://rustup.rs) (makasudin MSVC) tare da kayan aikin gina Visual Studio C++, da lokacin aiki na WebView2.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/leon03131/Decoy.git
cd Decoy
npm install

npm run tauri dev      # gudanar a yanayin ci gaba tare da sake lodawa mai zafi
npm run tauri build    # samar da mai sakawa a cikin src-tauri/target/release/bundle/nsis
```

Duba masu amfani:

```bash
npm run check-locales  # inganta kowane fayil ɗin fassara akan ru.json
npx tsc --noEmit       # duba nau'in frontend
cargo check --manifest-path src-tauri/Cargo.toml
```

Bayanan kula: ana haɗa decoy exe ta `src-tauri/build.rs` tare da bayyananniyar `rustc` daga `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs` sannan a saka shi a cikin app ɗin. Lokacin da frontend ke gudana a cikin burauza na yau da kullun (`npm run dev`) ana amfani da ƙaramin mock backend (`src/mock.ts`), wanda yake da amfani don aiki akan UI.

## Fassarori

Rubutun UI suna zaune a cikin [`src/locales`](../../src/locales), fayil ɗin JSON ɗaya a kowane yare (`en.json`, `ru.json`, ...).
Ƙara yare yana nufin ƙara fayil; babu canjin lamba. Duba [docs/TRANSLATING.md](../TRANSLATING.md) don tsarin fayil, buƙatar shirye-shiryen amfani don fassarar na'ura da rubutun ingantawa. Fassarorin wannan README suna zuwa `docs/i18n/README.<code>.md`.

## Tsarin aiki

```
src/                    Frontend na React + TypeScript
  components/           Dakin karatu, allon daki-daki, saituna, ...
  locales/              fayilolin fassara (JSON guda ɗaya a kowane yare)
  i18n-core.ts          lodawa da binciken yare
  store.ts              halin aikace-aikacen (zustand)
src-tauri/              Backend na Rust (Tauri 2)
  src/lib.rs            bayanan wasa, hanyoyin decoy, tire
  stub/stub.rs          shirin maye gurbi da aka saka
scripts/                rubutun mataimaka (inganta yanki)
docs/                   jagorar fassara, hotunan allo
```

## Sirri

- Decoy yana amfani da hanyar sadarwa ne kawai don saukar da jerin abubuwan ganowa na Discord na fili (a farkon farawa, sannan aƙalla sau ɗaya a rana ko lokacin da kuka danna sabuntawa) kuma, don nunawa, gumakan wasa da zane-zane daga CDN na jama'a na Discord.
- Ba ya karantawa, adanawa ko aika token ɗinku na Discord ko kowane bayanan asusu, kuma ba ya magana da abokin ciniki na Discord.
- Ana adana saituna (abubuwan da aka fi so, yare, tsawon lokaci) a cikin ma'ajiyar WebView ta app; ma'ajiyar jerin wasannin da fayilolin decoy na wucin gadi suna zaune a cikin `%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.decoy.desktop`.
- Babu bincike (analytics), babu rahoton faɗuwa, babu asusu.

## Sanannun iyakoki

- Discord na iya canza yadda ganowa ke aiki a kowane lokaci. Idan decoy ya daina gane shi, da fatan za a buɗe matsala (issue).
- Wasu ayyukan suna buƙatar fiye da tsari mai gudana (misali kasancewa a cikin wani ƙwarewa na dandalin wasa). Decoy yana yin koyi da tsarin ne kawai.
- Ba za a iya gano shigarwar da ba su da fayiloli masu aiwatarwa a cikin jerin Discord ba.
- Manhajar tsaro a wasu lokuta tana sanya tuta a kan ƙananan fayiloli masu aiwatarwa marasa sa hannu waɗanda ke zaune ba tare da aiki ba a ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya. Decoy shine buɗaɗɗen tushe kuma an gina shi daga `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs`; zaku iya haɗa shi da kanku.

## Gudummawa

Ana maraba da rahotannin kurakurai, ra'ayoyi da fassarori. Da fatan za a karanta [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) da farko.

## Gargaɗi

Ana ba da Decoy "kamar yadda yake" don dalilai na sirri, ilimi da gwaji. Yin koyi da wasan da ke gudana na iya keta Sharuɗɗan Sabis na Discord ko na mawallafin wasan, kuma yana iya haifar da mataki a kan asusunku. Kai kaɗai ne ke da alhakin yadda kake amfani da shi. "Discord" da duk sunayen wasanni da tambari alamun kasuwanci ne na masu su kuma ana amfani da su anan ne kawai don gano software ɗin da suke komawa zuwa gare ta.
