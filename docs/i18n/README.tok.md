# Decoy

**ilo tawa lili pi ike ala: ona li pana e pilin tawa Discord e ni: musi li tawa.**

![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Windows%2010%20%7C%2011-7c6cf6)
![Built with](https://img.shields.io/badge/built%20with-Tauri%202%20%2B%20Rust-7c6cf6)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-7c6cf6)

<img src="../screenshots/library.jpg" width="860" alt="poki musi pi ilo Decoy en musi sin en sitelen" />

</div>

ilo Decoy li awen tawa e ilo lili pi ike ala kepeken lipu lili pi lukin ala lon nimi pi ilo exe pi musi. tan ni la ilo Discord li toki e ni: sina musi e musi ni. sina wile ala pana e musi lon ilo sina, sina wile ala open e musi lon. ijo suli ala li awen lon ilo lipu sina.

ona li sona e **musi ale pi ken alasa Discord** (musi 25 000 samin), tan ni: ona li lukin e lipu kulupu pi ilo Discord, li sin e ona lon tenpo ale.

> [!WARNING]
> pali e tawa musi lon ala li ken pakala e Lawa Kepeken pi Discord. sina kepeken ilo Decoy **kepeken wile en sona pi sina taso**.
> kulupu pi ilo ni li kulupu Discord Inc. ala, li jo ala e wawa tan ona. o lukin e [toki pi sona wawa](#toki-pi-sona-wawa).

## ken pi ilo ni

- **musi ale pi ken alasa Discord.** lipu musi li kama tan lipu kulupu Discord, li awen lon ilo sina, li sin lon tenpo suno ale. musi sin en ilo exe sin li kama sin tan ona sama.
- **alasa wawa.** alasa kepeken nimi musi anu nimi exe lon musi mute mute; lukin leko anu lukin linja; musi olin en linja pi "musi pi tenpo pini lili".
- **pilin wan taso.** o alasa e musi, o pana e ilo exe (ilo exe mute li lon la), o pana e suli tenpo, o open. sike tenpo en toki ilo li pana e sona lon pini tenpo.
- **ona li weka e ijo ale.** ilo Decoy li lon lon tenpo tawa taso: ilo exe lili li kama lon open, li weka lon pini. sina pini e ilo Decoy suli la ilo ale li pini. ilo li pakala la ona li weka e ijo awen lon open kama.
- **kalama ala.** ona li awen lon poki anpa pi ilo Windows (system tray). lipu lukin ala li lon tawa ilo Decoy lili.
- **toki mute.** toki pi lipu lukin li lon lipu JSON taso. pana pi toki sin li wile e lipu wan taso. toki Inli en toki Losi li lon.
- **len pi sona sina.** lipu jan ala, nimi ken Discord ala, alasa pi sona kepeken ala. o lukin e [len pi sona sina](#len-pi-sona-sina).

## nasin pali

1. Discord li toki e ni: sina musi e musi, tan ni: ona li lukin e ilo tawa lon ilo Windows sina, li ante e nimi ilo tawa tawa lipu kulupu (`/api/v9/applications/detectable`).
2. ilo Decoy li kama jo e lipu ni. musi pi wile sina la ona li pana e ilo tawa lili (120 KB samin, li pali kepeken Rust, li awen insa ilo Decoy) tawa poki pi tenpo lili kepeken nimi exe tan lipu ni, sama `.../cod26-cod.exe`.
3. ilo lili ni li pali e ala, li jo taso e lipu lukin wan lon weka suli pi ilo lukin sina, tawa ni: sina ken ala lukin e ona. Discord li ken taso lukin e ilo tawa pi lipu lukin. ilo tawa pi lipu lukin ala li ken ala pana e sona.
4. lon tenpo lili (tenpo lili 15 samin) Discord li pana lukin e musi ni lon nimi sina.
5. tenpo li pini anu sina pilin e pini la ilo Decoy li pini e ilo tawa, li weka e lipu exe. ilo lawa pi Windows job object li pini e ilo Decoy lili lon pini pi Decoy suli, kin lon tenpo pakala.

## kama jo

o kama jo e lipu tan [lipu sin](../../../../releases):

| lipu | tawa |
|---|---|
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` | **ilo pana.** tawa jan wan taso, li wile ala e wawa lawa suli, li pana e nimi lon lipu open (Start menu) e ilo weka. |
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-portable.zip` | **ilo tawa tomo ante.** o open lon tomo lipu ale, o tawa e `decoy.exe`. sona ale li awen lon poki `data` poka ona. ona li sitelen ala lon poki pi jan sina. o weka e poki lipu tawa weka ona. |

o lukin e pona lipu kepeken `SHA256SUMS.txt` (`Get-FileHash <file>` lon ilo PowerShell).

ilo Windows SmartScreen li ken pana e toki pi sona ala tan ni: lipu li jo ala e sitelen lawa. o pilin e *More info → Run anyway* lon tenpo ni: sina pilin e pona ona.

wile ilo: Windows 10 anu Windows 11 (64-bit) en ilo WebView2 (ona li lon Windows 11 en Windows 10 sin).

## nasin kepeken

1. o open e **poki musi** (Library), o alasa e musi, anu o pilin e nena `Ctrl` + `K` lon ma ale.
2. o pilin e musi. lon poki poka o pana e ilo exe e mute tenpo (wile musi pi Discord li wile e tenpo lili 15 lon tenpo mute).
3. o pilin e **open e Decoy** (Start decoy). Discord li lukin e musi lon tenpo lili kama.
4. o lukin e ona lon **tawa lon** (Running). sina ken pini e wan anu ale lon tenpo ale, anu sina ken awen e Decoy lon poki anpa.

musi pi ilo exe ala lon lipu Discord li len lon tenpo open (Discord li ken ala lukin e ona). o open e *pana lukin e musi pi ilo exe ala* (Show games without exe) tawa lukin e ona. lipu li sin lon tenpo suno ale, la ona li ken tawa lon tenpo ni: Discord li pana e ilo exe.

### ilo toki pi linja sitelen

```
decoy.exe --launch "<nasin ilo exe tan lipu Discord>" [tenpo lili]
```

ona li open wawa e Decoy, sama ni: `decoy.exe --launch "robloxplayerbeta.exe" 15`.

## pali tan lipu mama

wile: [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 20+, ilo [Rust](https://rustup.rs) (MSVC) en ilo Visual Studio C++, en ilo WebView2.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/leon03131/Decoy.git
cd Decoy
npm install

npm run tauri dev      # open lon nasin pali kepeken sin wawa
npm run tauri build    # pali e ilo pana lon src-tauri/target/release/bundle/nsis
```

ilo pi lukin pona:

```bash
npm run check-locales  # lukin e pona pi lipu toki ale tawa ru.json
npx tsc --noEmit       # lukin e pona pi lipu frontend
cargo check --manifest-path src-tauri/Cargo.toml
```

sona lili: ilo `src-tauri/build.rs` li kepeken `rustc` li pali e ilo decoy exe tan `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs`, li pana e ona insa ilo Decoy. ilo frontend li tawa lon ilo lukin lipu taso (`npm run dev`) la ilo `src/mock.ts` li pana e sona nasa pona tawa pali pi lipu lukin.

## toki ante

toki pi lipu lukin li lon [`src/locales`](../../src/locales), lipu JSON wan tawa toki wan (`en.json`, `ru.json`, ...). pana pi toki sin li pana pi lipu wan taso; ante ala lon ilo pali. o lukin e [docs/TRANSLATING.md](../TRANSLATING.md) tawa sona nasin lipu en toki tawa ilo ante en ilo pi lukin pona. lipu toki ante pi README ni li tawa `docs/i18n/README.<code>.md`.

## ma pi lipu ale

```
src/                    ilo lukin pi React + TypeScript
  components/           poki musi, lipu lili, ante, ...
  locales/              lipu toki (lipu JSON wan tawa toki wan)
  i18n-core.ts          kama jo en alasa pi toki
  store.ts              awen pi sona ilo (zustand)
src-tauri/              ilo anpa pi Rust (Tauri 2)
  src/lib.rs            poki sona pi musi, ilo Decoy, poki anpa
  stub/stub.rs          ilo lili pi awen insa
scripts/                ilo lili pi pali pona (lukin e lipu toki)
docs/                   lipu pi pana toki, sitelen lukin
```

## len pi sona sina

- ilo Decoy li kepeken linja kulupu tawa kama jo pi lipu kulupu Discord taso (lon open nanpa wan, li tenpo wan taso lon tenpo suno wan anu lon tenpo ni: sina pilin e sin) en sitelen musi tan lipu CDN pi Discord.
- ona li lukin ala, li awen ala, li pana ala e nimi ken Discord sina anu sona pi lipu jan sina, li toki ala tawa ilo Discord.
- sona ante (musi olin, toki, tenpo) li awen lon poki ilo WebView lon ilo sina; lipu musi en lipu Decoy pi tenpo lili li awen lon `%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.decoy.desktop`.
- alasa pi sona kepeken ala, toki pakala ala, lipu jan ala.

## pakala pi ken ilo

- Discord li ken ante e nasin alasa lon tenpo ale. ilo Decoy li pini lukin la o open e lipu pakala (issue).
- musi ante li wile e ijo ante (sama ni: jan li lon insa musi pi ilo suli musi). Decoy li pali e tawa pi ilo tawa taso.
- musi pi ilo exe ala lon lipu Discord li ken ala alasa.
- ilo pi awen ilo (antivirus) li ken toki ike lon ilo exe lili pi lape lon insa ilo. ilo Decoy li jo e lipu mama open, li kama tan `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs`; sina ken pali e ona kepeken ilo sina.

## pana pona

lipu pakala en pilin sin en toki ante li pona. o lukin e [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) lon tenpo open.

## toki pi sona wawa

ilo Decoy li lon tawa kepeken pi sina taso, tawa kama sona, tawa alasa pona. pali e tawa musi lon ala li ken pakala e Lawa Kepeken pi Discord anu pi jan pali musi, li ken weka e lipu jan sina. sina taso li lawa e kepeken sina. nimi "Discord" en nimi musi ale en sitelen musi ale li ijo pi jan lawa ona, li lon ni tawa ni taso: pana e sona pi ilo ona.
