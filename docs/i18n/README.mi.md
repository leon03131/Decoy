# Decoy

**He hōtaka pupuri iti e whakapono ai a Discord kei te rere tētahi kēmu.**

![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Windows%2010%20%7C%2011-7c6cf6)
![Built with](https://img.shields.io/badge/built%20with-Tauri%202%20%2B%20Rust-7c6cf6)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-7c6cf6)

<img src="../screenshots/library.jpg" width="860" alt="Pātengi kēmu Decoy me ngā kēmu nō tata nei me ngā toi" />

</div>

Ka pupuri a Decoy i tētahi hōtaka whakarite harakore, whai matapihi hoki e rere ana i raro i te ingoa kōnae kawe o tētahi kēmu, kia whakaatu ai te kitenga kēmu a Discord kei te purei koe i taua kēmu. Kāore he take kia tāuta, kia whakarewa rānei koe i te tino kēmu, nō reira kāore he mea nui e noho ana i tō kōpae mārō.

E mōhio ana a Decoy ki **ngā kēmu katoa ka taea e Discord te kite** (tata ki te 25,000 ngā urunga) nā te mea ka pānuitia te rārangi kitenga tūmatanui tonu a Discord, ā, ka whakahou aunoa i a ia anō.

> [!WARNING]
> Tērā pea ka takahi te whaiwhakaaro i tētahi kēmu e rere ana i ngā Whakaritenga Ratonga a Discord, ā, ka whakamahi koe i a Decoy **i raro i tō ake tūponotanga**.
> Kāore tēnei kaupapa i te hono atu ki, i tautokona e, i pā rānei ki a Discord Inc. Tirohia te [Whakakāhoretanga Whakatūpato](#whakakāhoretanga-whakatūpato).

## Ngā Āhuatanga Motuhake

- **Ngā mea katoa ka taea e Discord te kite.** Ka tīkina te rārangi kēmu mai i te pūwāhi kitenga tūmatanui a Discord, ka keterokititia ā-rohe, ā, ka whakahoutia ia rā. Ka puta noa mai ngā kēmu hōu me ngā kōnae kawe hōu.
- **Rapunga tere tonu.** He rapunga tere mā te ingoa kēmu, kōnae kawe rānei puta noa i ngā mano tini kēmu, he tirohanga pouaka, he rārangi kiato rānei, he kēmu makau, me tētahi rārangi "Inā tata nei".
- **Pāwhiri kotahi.** Kōwhiria he kēmu, kōwhiria te kōnae kawe (mehemea he maha), whakaritea te roa o te pupuri, ka pēhi tīmata. Mā te porowhita tatau-iho me te whakamōhiotanga koe e whakamōhio ina pau te wā.
- **Kāore he toenga e waiho.** Ka ora noa iho he decoy i te wā e rere ana: ka hangaia te exe iti i te tīmatanga, ā, ka mukua ina mutu. Ka whakakorea ngā mea katoa ina puta koe, ā, ki te hinga ohorere te hōtaka ka tahia ngā toenga i te whakarewatanga ka whai ake.
- **Hūmarie.** E noho ana ki te pae pūnaha (system tray), kāore he matapihi, kāore hoki he pātene pae mahi mō ngā decoy tonu.
- **Whakamāoritanga tuwhera.** E whakahaerehia ana te atanga e ngā kōnae JSON māmā, nō reira he kōnae kotahi noa iho hei tāpiri reo hōu. Kei roto kē te reo Pākehā me te reo Rūhia.
- **Tūmataiti.** Kāore he kaute, kāore he tohu Discord, kāore he aroturuki raraunga. Tirohia te [Tūmataiti](#tūmataiti).

## Me pēhea te mahi

1. Ka whakatau a Discord kei te purei koe i tētahi kēmu mā te tirotiro i ngā hōtaka e rere ana i tō rorohiko, me te whakataurite i ō rātou ingoa kōnae kawe ki tētahi rārangi tūmatanui (`/api/v9/applications/detectable`).
2. Ka tīkina e Decoy taua rārangi, ā, mō te kēmu e kōwhiria ana e koe, ka tāruatia he hōtaka whakakapi iti rawa (tata ki te 120 KB, i tuhia ki te Rust, ā, kua whakaurua ki roto i a Decoy) ki tētahi kōpaki rangitahi mā te whakamahi i te ingoa kōnae kawe mai i te rārangi, hei tauira `.../cod26-cod.exe`.
3. Kāore he mahi a te hōtaka whakakapi heoi anō ko te pupuri i tētahi matapihi taumata-runga kotahi kua waihotia ki tawhiti i waho o te mata kia kore ai koe e kite. Ka mōhio noa iho a Discord ki tētahi hōtaka he matapihi tōna; koia rā te take ka waiho noatia he hōtaka kāore kau he matapihi.
4. I roto i te 15 hēkona pea, ka whakaatu a Discord i taua kēmu hei ngohe ōu ināianei.
5. Ina mutu te taima, ka pēhi rānei koe i te whakamutu, ka whakamutua e Decoy te hōtaka me te muku i te kōnae. Mā tētahi ahanoa mahi Windows (job object) e whakaū ka hemo ngā decoy i te taha o Decoy, ahakoa he hinga ohorere nōna.

## Tāutanga

Kōwhiria tētahi mai i te whārangi [Tuku Putanga](../../../../releases):

| Tikiake | Mō |
|---|---|
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` | **Kaitāuta.** Mō ia kaiwhakamahi, kāore e hiahiatia ngā motika kaiwhakahaere, ka tāpiri urunga ki te Tahua Tīmata me tētahi kaimuku tāutanga. |
| `Decoy_x.y.z_x64-portable.zip` | **Kawe.** Wetewetehia ki hea noa iho ka whakahaere i a `decoy.exe`. Ka noho ngā raraunga katoa ki te kōpaki `data` i tōna taha, kāore he mea e tuhia ki tō kōtaha kaiwhakamahi. Mukua te kōpaki hei tango atu. |

Whakatūturu he tikiakenga mā te `SHA256SUMS.txt` (`Get-FileHash <kōnae>` i roto i te PowerShell).

Tērā pea ka whakatūpato a Windows SmartScreen mō tētahi kaiwhakaputa kāore i te mōhiotia i te mea kāore anō kia hainatia ā-whakarorohikotia ngā hanganga; kōwhiria *More info → Run anyway* mehemea e whakapono ana koe ki te hanganga.

Ngā hiahia pūnaha: Windows 10, 11 rānei (64-bit) me te taiao whakahaere WebView2 (kua whakaurua kē ki Windows 11 me ngā whakahōutanga o mua tata nei o Windows 10).

## Whakamahinga

1. Huakina te **Pātengi Kēmu** ka rapu kēmu, pēhia rānei te `Ctrl` + `K` mai i hea noa atu.
2. Pāwhiria he kēmu. I te pae kei te taha matau kōwhiria te hōtaka me te maha o ngā meneti hei pupuri (ko ngā ngohe a Discord me 15 meneti te tikanga).
3. Pēhia te **Tīmata decoy**. Ka whakaatu a Discord i te kēmu i muri i ētahi hēkona torutoru.
4. Tirohia i raro i te **E rere ana**, whakamutua kia kotahi, ngā mea katoa rānei i ngā wā katoa, waiho noa iho rānei a Decoy i te pae pūnaha.

Ko ngā kēmu kāore anō kia whai kōnae kawe i roto i te urunga Discord ka hunaia ā-taunoatia (kāore e taea te kite). Whakakāngia te *Whakaaturia ngā kēmu kāore he exe* kia kitea ai rātou; ka whakahoutia te rārangi ia rā, kia wātea tonu mai i te wā ka tāpiri a Discord i tētahi hōtaka.

### Raina whakahau

```
decoy.exe --launch "<ara kōnae kawe mai i te rārangi Discord>" [meneti]
```

Ka tīmata tonu i tētahi decoy, hei tauira `decoy.exe --launch "robloxplayerbeta.exe" 15`.

## Hanga mai i te puna wetewete

Ngā whakaritenga o mua: [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 20+, te huinga taputapu pūmau o [Rust](https://rustup.rs) (whāinga MSVC) me ngā taputapu hanga Visual Studio C++, me te taiao whakahaere WebView2.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/leon03131/Decoy.git
cd Decoy
npm install

npm run tauri dev      # whakahaere i te aratau whanaketanga me te uta-wera
npm run tauri build    # waihanga i te kaitāuta ki src-tauri/target/release/bundle/nsis
```

Ngā arowhai whaihua:

```bash
npm run check-locales  # whakatūturu i ia kōnae whakamāoritanga ki te ru.json
npx tsc --noEmit       # tirohia ngā momo tātari o te mata o mua
cargo check --manifest-path src-tauri/Cargo.toml
```

Ngā tuhipoka: ka whakahiatotia te decoy exe e `src-tauri/build.rs` mā te `rustc` noa iho mai i `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs`, ā, ka whakaurua ki roto i te taupānga. Ina rere te mata o mua i roto i tētahi pūtirotiro noa (`npm run dev`), ka whakamahia he tuara hangarau whakamātautau iti (`src/mock.ts`), he mea pai hei mahi i te atanga kaiwhakamahi.

## Whakamāoritanga

Kei [`src/locales`](../../src/locales) ngā aho kōrero atanga, kotahi te kōnae JSON mō ia reo (`en.json`, `ru.json`, ...). Ko te tāpiri reo he tāpiri kōnae noa iho; kāore he panonitanga waehere. Tirohia te [docs/TRANSLATING.md](../TRANSLATING.md) mō te whakatakotoranga kōnae, tētahi akiaki kua rite mō te whakamāori māhini me te tuhinga arowhai. Ko ngā whakamāoritanga o tēnei README ka haere ki `docs/i18n/README.<code>.md`.

## Tahora Kaupapa

```
src/                    Mata o mua React + TypeScript
  components/           Pātengi kēmu, pae taipitopito, tautuhinga, ...
  locales/              kōnae whakamāoritanga (kotahi JSON mō ia reo)
  i18n-core.ts          tukuatu me te rapu reo
  store.ts              āhua o te taupānga (zustand)
src-tauri/              Tuarā Rust (Tauri 2)
  src/lib.rs            pātengi raraunga kēmu, hōtaka decoy, pae pūnaha
  stub/stub.rs          te hōtaka whakakapi ka whakaurua
scripts/                ngā tuhinga āwhina (arowhai reo)
docs/                   aratohu whakamāori, whakaahua mata
```

## Tūmataiti

- Ka whakamahi noa a Decoy i te whatunga ki te tikiake i te rārangi kitenga tūmatanui a Discord (i te whakarewatanga tuatahi, ā, kāore e neke atu i te kotahi i te rā, i te wā rānei e pēhi ana koe i te whakahou) ā, mō te whakaatu, ngā ata kēmu me ngā toi mai i te CDN tūmatanui a Discord.
- Kāore ia e pānui, e penapena, e tuku rānei i tō tohu Discord me ētahi raraunga kaute, ā, kāore hoki e kōrero ki te kiritaki Discord.
- Ka penapenahia ngā tautuhinga (kēmu makau, reo, roanga wā) ki roto i te rokiroki WebView o te taupānga i runga i te rorohiko; ka noho te keteroki rārangi kēmu me ngā kōnae decoy rangitahi ki `%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.decoy.desktop`.
- Kāore he aroturuki wetewete raraunga, kāore he pūrongo tukinga, kāore he kaute.

## Ngā herenga e mōhiotia ana

- Ka taea e Discord te whakarerekē i te mahinga o te kitenga i ngā wā katoa. Ki te kore tētahi decoy e kitea anō, tēnā koa whakatuwheratia he take (issue).
- Ko ētahi ngohe me whai mea kē atu i te hōtaka e rere ana (hei tauira, te noho ki roto i tētahi wheako pūmau o tētahi tūāpapa kēmu). Ka whakatauira noa a Decoy i te hōtaka.
- Ko ngā urunga kāore he kōnae kawe i roto i te rārangi a Discord kāore e taea te kite.
- I ētahi wā ka tohua e te pūmanawa haumaru ngā kōnae kawe iti kāore i hainatia e noho mangere ana i roto i te mahara. He puna tuwhera te decoy, ā, kua hangaia mai i `src-tauri/stub/stub.rs`; ka taea e koe anō te whakahiatotanga.
